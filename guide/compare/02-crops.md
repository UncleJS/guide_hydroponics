## Introduction

NFT is the better tool for high-turnover leafy crops and compact herbs. Ebb and Flow opens cucumber, zucchini (courgette), eggplant (aubergine), and full-size vine tomatoes. Cherry tomato and pepper can sit on either the NFT **CH4 fruiting tank** or an Ebb and Flow table. Zone B (microgreens) and Zone C (grow bags) are the same in both builds.

## 1. Why system design sets the crop list

### Root mass and support

This NFT build uses four **8 ft (2.44 m)** channels: CH1–CH3 are **3 in (76 mm)** with **2 in (51 mm)** net pots at **9 in (229 mm)**; CH4 is **4 in (102 mm)** with **3 in (76 mm)** pots at **12 in (305 mm)**. Forty sites total. Roots hang in a thin film. That limits:

- **Root mass** — a cucumber or beef tomato fills a channel and blocks flow
- **Plant weight** — the net pot rim carries the canopy; heavy vines need stakes tied to the frame, not to the channel alone
- **Shared EC** — CH1–CH3 share one **20 US gal (76 L)** greens tank at **0.8–1.8 mS/cm**. CH4 has its own **10 US gal (38 L)** tank so fruiting EC never lands on lettuce

Ebb and Flow uses three **4 ft × 2 ft (1.22 m × 0.61 m)** tables filled with **5 in (13 cm)** of LECA — **25 US gal (95 L)** per table. Roots explore the bed. Stakes and cages sit in the media. One **45 US gal (170 L)** reservoir feeds all three tables, so one EC serves the whole Zone A.

### Water stress

Both NFT pumps run **24 hours a day**. There is no overnight off. In warm weather a stopped channel dries in **15–30 minutes**.

Ebb and Flow floods **3 times a day** in vegetative growth and **4 times a day** in fruiting. Four is the ceiling. Between floods, moist LECA buffers **8–24 hours**. Do not plan on wilt in 90 minutes after one missed flood.

### Nutrient rhythm

Leafy crops want steady nitrogen. Continuous NFT flow on the greens loop suits that. Fruiting crops want higher EC and more potassium and calcium later. On NFT that means the CH4 tank. On Ebb and Flow that means raising the shared tank toward the fruiting crop and keeping Table 3 on leafy crops only when the tank is still in a leafy band, or accepting a compromise EC when fruiting starts.

## 2. NFT crop library

### Excellent on the greens loop (CH1–CH3)

| Channel | Crop | Sites | EC (mS/cm) | Notes |
|---------|------|-------|------------|-------|
| CH1 | Lettuce | 11 | 0.8–1.8 (ceiling 1.8) | Best NFT crop; 4–6 weeks from transplant |
| CH2 | Basil, cilantro (coriander), parsley, chives | 11 | 1.0–1.8 | Cut-and-come-again; succession basil every 2–3 weeks |
| CH3 | Spinach, kale, mint | 11 | 0.8–1.8 | Cooler crops prefer spring and fall |
| CH3 | Strawberries | 3–4 of the 11 | greens EC | Never on CH4 |

Both pumps continuous. Change the greens tank every **7 days**.

### Excellent on the fruiting loop (CH4)

| Crop | Plants | EC fruiting | Notes |
|------|--------|-------------|-------|
| Cherry tomato | 4–5 indeterminate (skip holes) or up to 7 compact | 2.5–3.5 | Own 10 US gal (38 L) tank and pump |
| Pepper | same holes | 2.0–3.0 | Same tank as cherry tomato if you run one fruiting crop at a time, or pick one EC band |

Change the fruiting tank every **5–7 days**. Planning yield: **4–6 lb (1.8–2.7 kg)** cherry tomatoes per plant.

### Not in the NFT channels

Cucumber, zucchini (courgette), eggplant (aubergine), beef/vine tomato that needs a deep bed, potatoes, and all true root vegetables. Carrots, radish, and beet (beetroot) go in **Zone C grow bags**, not in channels.

## 3. Ebb and Flow crop library

### Table assignment

| Table | Crop | Plants | Floods |
|-------|------|--------|--------|
| Table 1 | Indeterminate tomato **or** cucumber | 1 | 3× veg → 4× fruiting |
| Table 2 | Pepper, eggplant (aubergine), or zucchini (courgette) | 1–2 | 3× → 4× |
| Table 3 | Lettuce, herbs, pak choi, or a later fruiting crop (strawberries here) | Several | 3× (4× only if fruiting) |

Flood duration **15–30 minutes**. Do not add a fifth flood. In heat above **85°F (29°C)**, keep 4 floods and add **40% shade**.

### Excellent in Ebb and Flow

**Tomato (Table 1 or, for cherry only, NFT CH4)**  
Reservoir EC fruiting **2.5–3.5 mS/cm**. Flush LECA if media EC is more than **0.5 mS/cm** above the reservoir; treat **+1.0** as urgent.

**Cucumber (Table 1)**  
One plant per table. Reservoir EC about **2.2–2.8 mS/cm**. Train vertically.

**Zucchini (courgette) (Table 2)**  
One plant. EC about **1.8–2.4 mS/cm**. Canopy spills past the table edge.

**Eggplant (aubergine) (Table 2)**  
1–2 plants. Warm nights; EC about **2.0–2.4 mS/cm**.

**Pepper (Table 2 or NFT CH4)**  
1–2 plants per table. Fruiting EC **2.0–3.0 mS/cm**, not 1.8–2.2.

**Strawberry (Table 3 or NFT CH3)**  
Greens EC **1.2–1.6 mS/cm**. Crowns stay above the flood line.

**Lettuce and herbs (Table 3)**  
Work, but NFT denser for the same footprint. Keep floods at 3× unless the shared tank is already on a fruiting schedule.

### Not in the flood tables

Root vegetables (Zone C bags), potatoes, watercress (needs continuous flow — use NFT if you grow it).

## 5. Yield estimates

Planning figures for inland mid-USA, about 38°N, summer clear-sky DLI about **45–55 mol/m²/day**, season mid-April through mid-October (SA: mid-October through mid-April). Shade at **40%** when highs hold above **85°F (29°C)**.

### Leafy and herbs (NFT greens loop)

| Crop | Days transplant to harvest | Sites | Notes |
|------|----------------------------|-------|-------|
| Butterhead lettuce | 28–35 | 11 on CH1 | Continuous succession |
| Spinach / kale | 28–40 | CH3 | Prefer spring and fall |
| Basil | first cut 21–28 | CH2 | Succession every 2–3 weeks |
| Cilantro | 21–28 | CH2 | Short window; re-sow often |

### Fruiting

| Crop | System | Planning yield |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Cherry tomato | NFT CH4 or Ebb and Flow Table 1 | **4–6 lb (1.8–2.7 kg)** per plant |
| Pepper | NFT CH4 or Table 2 | 20–40 fruits per plant (variety) |
| Cucumber | Table 1 | 8–15 fruits |
| Zucchini (courgette) | Table 2 | 15–30 fruits |
| Eggplant (aubergine) | Table 2 | 6–10 fruits |

### Zone B and Zone C (identical in both builds)

| Zone | Planning yield |
|------|----------------|
| Zone B microgreens | Many 7–14 day trays; pH 5.8–6.2 water only (sunflower and pea may use EC 0.4–0.8) |
| Zone C | Radish **15 lb (6.8 kg)**, beet **8 lb (3.6 kg)**, carrot **20 lb (9.1 kg)** — bags two 5 US gal radish, one 5 US gal beet, three 10 US gal carrot |

## 6. Succession planning

Months are US dates. South African months sit six months later in brackets.

### NFT (Zone A channels)

| Month | CH1 | CH2 | CH3 | CH4 |
|-------|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| January (SA: July) | Off outdoors | Off | Off | Off |
| February (SA: August) | Indoor seedlings | Indoor seedlings | Indoor seedlings | Indoor seedlings |
| March (SA: September) | Indoor starts | Indoor starts | Indoor starts | Indoor starts |
| April (SA: October) | Transplant after ~April 15 | Herbs after frost risk drops | Spinach, kale, 3–4 strawberry | Harden cherry / pepper |
| May–August (SA: November–February) | Lettuce succession | Herb cuts | Greens + strawberry | Fruiting EC on CH4 tank |
| September–October (SA: March–April) | Cool lettuces | Late herbs | Spinach, kale | Late fruit; clear before frost ~October 20 |
| November–December (SA: May–June) | Drain, clean, store | Same | Same | Same |

Zone B trays run whenever frost is not on the shelf. Zone C bags follow the same April–October outdoor window.

### Ebb and Flow (Zone A tables)

| Month | Table 1 | Table 2 | Table 3 |
|-------|---------|---------|---------|
| January–February | Off / indoor seed | Off / indoor seed | Off |
| March | Indoor tomato or cucumber | Indoor pepper / eggplant (aubergine) | Indoor leafy starts |
| Late April | Transplant fruiting vine | Transplant pepper / zucchini (courgette) | Lettuce / herbs / strawberry |
| May–August | Fruiting, 4 floods, 40% shade in heat | Fruiting, 4 floods | Leafy at 3 floods, or a late fruiting crop |
| September–October | Late fruit, then clear | Late fruit, then clear | Cool leafy |
| November–December | Clean LECA, store | Clean | Clean |

### Mixed systems

- NFT greens loop: all leafy and herbs, year-round density in season  
- NFT CH4 or Ebb and Flow Tables 1–2: cherry tomato and pepper  
- Ebb and Flow only: cucumber, zucchini (courgette), eggplant (aubergine)  
- Zone B and Zone C: always the shared designs

## 7. Spacing

### NFT (this build)

| Crop | Channel | Net pot | Hole spacing | Sites |
|------|---------|---------|--------------|-------|
| Lettuce | CH1 | 2 in (51 mm) | 9 in (229 mm) | 11 |
| Herbs | CH2 | 2 in (51 mm) | 9 in (229 mm) | 11 |
| Spinach, kale, mint, strawberry | CH3 | 2 in (51 mm) | 9 in (229 mm) | 11 (3–4 strawberry) |
| Cherry tomato, pepper | CH4 | 3 in (76 mm) | 12 in (305 mm) | 7 holes; plant 4–5 indeterminate or up to 7 compact |

### Ebb and Flow (per 4 ft × 2 ft table)

| Crop | Plants | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Tomato or cucumber | 1 | Table 1 |
| Pepper, eggplant (aubergine), zucchini (courgette) | 1–2 | Table 2 |
| Lettuce / herbs | 6–8 leafy or a herb mix | Table 3 |
| Strawberry | several | Table 3; crowns above flood |

LECA depth stays **5 in (13 cm)** for every table. Do not thin the bed to 1–1½ in for Mediterranean herbs; lengthen the dry interval instead (stay at 3 floods, not 5).

## 8. Transplanting

### Into NFT

1. Rockwool cubes about **1 in (25 mm)**. Soak in pH 5.5 water. No soil plugs.  
2. Germinate near **68–72°F (20–22°C)**.  
3. Transplant when the first true leaf shows and roots poke from the cube.  
4. Cube in net pot, clay pebbles around it, pot bottom a little above the film — not submerged.  
5. Confirm flow on that loop before you leave. Both pumps run 24 hours a day.

### Into Ebb and Flow

1. Rockwool or coco plugs.  
2. Transplant a little later (second true leaf) if you want.  
3. Pre-soak LECA 24 hours at pH **5.8**, fill to **5 in (13 cm)**, make a **1¼–2 in (3–5 cm)** pocket.  
4. First week: **3 floods/day** while roots establish. Move to **4×** only after a fruiting crop is established. Never a fifth flood.  
5. Open LECA bed — no lid holes for net pots.

Moving established plants between NFT and Ebb and Flow is a last resort. Expect a 3–7 day stall.

## 9. Problem hotspots

| Crop | System | Problem | Fix |
|------|--------|---------|-----|
| Lettuce | NFT | Tip burn | pH 5.8–6.2; greens EC not above 1.8; air movement |
| Lettuce | Ebb and Flow | Pythium | Full drain; stay at 3 floods; solution under 77°F (25°C) |
| Basil | NFT | Bolting | Succession; pinch flowers |
| Basil | Ebb and Flow | Fungus gnats | Do not over-flood; keep 3× |
| Tomato | Either fruiting loop / table | Blossom end rot | Calcium; media EC gap under 0.5; 4 floods in heat, not 5 |
| Cucumber | Ebb and Flow | Powdery mildew | Airflow; remove crowded leaves |
| Pepper | Either | Slow set | Night temps; fruiting EC 2.0–3.0 |
| Mint | NFT | Roots blocking CH3 | Large net pot; inspect return monthly |

## 10. Decision guide

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  want["What do you want to grow?"] --> fruit{"Fruiting crop?"}
  fruit -->|Cucumber zucchini (courgette) eggplant (aubergine)| efOnly["Ebb and Flow Tables 1 to 2"]
  fruit -->|Tomato or pepper| eitherFruit["Ebb and Flow table or NFT CH4 tank"]
  fruit -->|No| leafy{"Highest leafy density?"}
  leafy -->|Yes| nftGreens["NFT CH1 to CH3"]
  leafy -->|A few plants and Ebb and Flow already up| efTable3["Ebb and Flow Table 3"]
  leafy -->|Root veg| zoneC["Zone C grow bags"]
  leafy -->|Microgreens| zoneB["Zone B trays"]
  efOnly --> safety{"Digital timer and drain cutoff ready?"}
  eitherFruit --> safety
  safety -->|Yes| go["Plant"]
  safety -->|No| fix["Fit timer and stuck-ON cutoff first"]
  fix --> go
```

1. **Cucumber, zucchini (courgette), eggplant (aubergine)** → Ebb and Flow only.  
2. **Tomato and pepper** → Ebb and Flow table, or NFT CH4 (10 US gal / 38 L). Never the greens tank.  
3. **Lettuce, spinach, herbs** → NFT CH1–CH3 for density.  
4. **Strawberry** → NFT CH3 or Ebb and Flow Table 3. Not CH4.  
5. **Radish, carrot, beet** → Zone C bags (same both systems).  
6. **Microgreens** → Zone B (same both systems).
